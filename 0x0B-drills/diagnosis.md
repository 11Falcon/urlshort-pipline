# Diagnoses

One section per drill. At least 40 words each. Say **what was wrong** and **how
you proved it**, not just what you changed — the fix is already visible in the
workflow file; the reasoning is not.

A good entry answers three things: the symptom as seen from outside, the
mechanism that produced it, and the command or log line that confirmed it.
